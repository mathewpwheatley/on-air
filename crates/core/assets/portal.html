First time? Create a Spotify app
  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and create an app.
  2. Add this redirect URI: http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback
  3. Tick Web API, save, and copy the app's Client ID.

Approve in your browser. It then shows a page that can't load: copy that page's address and paste it here.

Then use your browser's Back button to come back here.
