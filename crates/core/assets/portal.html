Connected to the setup network

You need a free Spotify developer app:
  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and create an app.
  2. Add this redirect URI: http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback
  3. Tick Web API, save, and copy the app's Client ID.
Open Spotify sign-in

After you tap Agree, Spotify opens a page that won't load. That's expected. Copy that page's whole address and paste it here.
